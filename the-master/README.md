# The master

UMDCTF 2025, OSINT. A photo of a historical marker; the flag is its address.

## Solve

The marker is part of the John Hunt Morgan Heritage Trail. The Historical Marker Database
(hmdb.org) lists the whole series, so I walked its entries and matched the marker to number 41,
Lore City, in Guernsey County, Ohio. That marker stands on Main Street in Lore City.

## Flag

```text
UMDCTF{Main St, Lore City, OH 43755}
```

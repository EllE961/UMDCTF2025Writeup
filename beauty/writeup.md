# Beauty

## Challenge
> A stunning panoramic view was uploaded to Google Maps showing a beautiful natural vista. Who is the photographer behind this masterpiece? The answer might just be your ticket to the flag.

## Solution
I identified that the panorama was shot along the Scioto River.

Using the "browse street viewer" feature in Google Maps, I found that it was uploaded by Neil Larimore.

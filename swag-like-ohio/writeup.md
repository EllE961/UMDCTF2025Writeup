# Swag Like Ohio

## Challenge
> An image of an iconic bridge has been circulating online, but we need to pinpoint its exact location. The structure appears to be somewhere in Ohio. Can you identify the bridge and provide its complete address?

## Solution
After examining the image closely, I used Google Images reverse search to identify the structure.

The image shows the Putnam Street Bridge in Marietta, Ohio.

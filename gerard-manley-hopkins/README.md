# Gerard Manley Hopkins

UMDCTF 2025, OSINT. A street photo to geolocate down to an address.

## Solve

A garbage truck in the shot was lettered for Dailey's Recycling & Refuse, which operates around
Wellsville, Ohio. Following the street to the visible house numbers 1116 and 1121 placed it on
Hillcrest Road in Wellsville, OH 43968.
